Ever since you were little you were fascinated with all things magic. They called to you. Your mother used to call you her 'little fairy'. It was just something you always remember, the fascination, the interest, the thirst for knowledge and for... more.

But you'd never guess that the king, desperate for his land and his people because of the curse that looms and grabs ahold of his kingdom inch by inch each day... would send for you. The curse, from what you've heard in the letter was terrible. Bloody and merciless. Spreading across all the land, slowly turning its people into stone.

And so, after saying your goodbyes and packing up the few belonging you'd need and possess, you embark on the next ship to the Kingdom of Elaria, with the King's calling letter in your pocket, proof of his request... more similar to a demand, in all actuality. Since no one can defy a King. Not really. By the time you reach the village of Greybrook, in the outskirts of Elaria, dusk is already settling in. The air feels heavy — unnaturally still.

As you look around, taking in the strees, the people and their restlessness, the tension that fills the air seems to be coming from within the center of the square, where a small crowd has gathered. A man lies on the ground, his right arm completely turned to stone, the transformation creeping slowly toward his chest.

A woman kneels beside him, gripping his hand despite the cold marble skin. When she notices you watching, her eyes narrow — sharp, desperate. It is clear she's on the fence about your presence here. This town is certainly not welcomed to newcomers after all, especially not since after the curse.

"If you're here to stare, keep walking," she says coldly.

What do you do?
