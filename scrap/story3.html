"You didn't have to stay," she speaks, standing next to you and joining you where you stand, looking over the statues of people on the town's square.

She stands beside you in silence for a long moment, the two of you facing the square. Stone faces catch the torchlight — frozen expressions of fear, confusion, sometimes peace.

"I know," you answer quietly. The words feel small, inadequate — but honest.

Mira nods once. "Most don't."

"They used to laugh here," she continues softly, almost to herself. "Market days were loud. Children everywhere."

Her fingers curl into her palm as she exhales. "Now no one looks too long. As if that might invite it..."

Her gaze lingers on you for a moment longer before she looks back to the square. "If you're staying... you should know this isn't the first village."

"The curse comes in waves," she continues. "Sometimes weeks apart. Sometimes days... Always from the Capital... And it's reaching our borders now... our Shrine...
